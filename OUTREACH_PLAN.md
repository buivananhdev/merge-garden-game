Hi [Contact Name / Publishing Team],

[1-2 sentence hook about why this matters to THEM specifically]

[2-3 sentences about the game]

[Link to prototype]

[Call to action]

Best regards,
[Your Name]

## Talking Points for Calls

If you get a call scheduled:

1. **Lead with Market Opportunity**
   - "Merge games are a $500M+ category. This prototype demonstrates commercial viability."

2. **Highlight Production Quality**
   - "This isn't a concept—it's a working game with proven mechanics and monetization systems."

3. **Emphasize Risk Reduction**
   - "By starting with a validated prototype, we reduce development time and de-risk the launch."

4. **Offer Flexibility**
   - "I'm open to publishing, acquisition, co-dev, or investment structures that work best for your team."

5. **Ask Smart Questions**
   - "What does your ideal casual game pipeline look like?"
   - "How does this prototype fit into your current portfolio?"
   - "What would need to happen for this to be a strong fit for your team?"

## Final Notes

- **Personalization Matters:** Generic emails get ignored. Tailor 2-3 sentences per studio.
- **Follow-Up is Key:** Most deals happen on the 2nd or 3rd follow-up, not the first.
- **Be Professional but Direct:** You have something valuable. Act like it.
- **Move Fast:** If interest comes in, respond within 24 hours.
- **Document Everything:** Keep records of all conversations for future reference.
