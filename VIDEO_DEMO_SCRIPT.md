# Merge Garden – Video Demo Script (30 seconds)

## Visual Flow:

**0–3 seconds: Title & Hook**
- Show game title: "Merge Garden"
- Text overlay: "A casual merge puzzle game"
- Quick pan across the board showing colorful crops

**3–8 seconds: Gameplay Loop – Spawn**
- Camera on empty cell
- Tap to place seed (🌱)
- Show +15 score popup
- Voiceover: "Tap to spawn crops."

**8–15 seconds: Gameplay Loop – Merge**
- Show two adjacent seeds merging into 🌿
- Show merge animation and sound effect
- Show +35 score and +12 coins popups
- Voiceover: "Merge matching crops to evolve them."
- Repeat merge chain: 🌿 → 🌼 → 🍋 → 🍊

**15–20 seconds: Progression & Rewards**
- Show coins earned counter ticking up
- Show level progression from Level 1 → Level 2 → Level 3
- Show daily reward button being clicked: +30 coins
- Voiceover: "Earn coins. Progress through levels. Unlock rewards."

**20–28 seconds: Upgrades & Strategy**
- Show shop with three upgrades (Water, Soil, Luck)
- Click upgrade button, level up Water
- Show boost button activation
- Voiceover: "Buy upgrades. Activate boosts. Build your strategy."

**28–30 seconds: Call to Action**
- Show final game screenshot with score = 500+
- Text overlay: "Merge Garden – Play Now"
- Text overlay: "Available on [Web / App Store / Google Play]"
- Final voiceover: "Merge Garden. A casual puzzle experience."

---

## Audio:
- Background music: Light, upbeat casual game music (royalty-free)
- Sound effects: Merge chimes, reward pings, upgrade success tones
- Voiceover: Calm, friendly, energetic tone
- Total duration: 30 seconds

---

## Tips for Recording:
1. Use screen recording on desktop (OBS, ScreenFlow, or built-in recorder)
2. Record at 1080p or higher for clarity
3. Add text overlays in post-production (Adobe Premiere, CapCut, or DaVinci Resolve)
4. Keep cuts fast and smooth (0.5–1 second per action)
5. Overlay coins and score popups in editing if not visible in raw footage
