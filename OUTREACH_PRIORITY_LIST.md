## Best outreach pattern

### Always include:
- 1 sentence about why the studio is relevant
- 1 sentence about the game itself
- Link to project demo
- CTA: "happy to share gameplay video / deck / technical details"

### Avoid:
- Long sales copy
- Too much detail in the first email
- Multiple paragraphs about your personal background
- Sending without a clear CTA

---

## Follow-up schedule

### If no response after 3-5 days:
Send FOLLOWUP_EMAIL.md

### If no response after 7-10 days:
Send a second follow-up with a shorter tone:
- "Just checking if this is something your team might review."
- "Happy to send a gameplay video or quick deck if helpful."

---

## Goal

The goal is not to send to everyone at once. The goal is to send the right emails to the most relevant studios with a clean pitch and then follow up professionally.

This is the fastest path from prototype to potential partnership.
