## Game Overview & Market Fit

### The Game
Merge Garden is a casual puzzle game where:
- Players spawn seeds on a 5x5 grid
- Merging matching crops evolves them (🌱 → 🌿 → 🌼 → 🍊 → 🌳 → 💎 → ✨)
- Every merge earns coins and score
- Coins buy upgrades (Water, Soil, Luck) that boost rewards
- Players level up and unlock new progression goals

**Gameplay Loop:** Spawn → Merge → Earn → Upgrade → Level → Repeat

This loop is addictive, satisfying, and designed for both 5-minute sessions and extended play.

## Core Mechanics – Why It Works

### 1. Spawn (Player Agency)
Players tap empty cells to place seeds. Simple action, immediate feedback. No artificial limits—just a board management puzzle.

**Monetization Hook:** "Boost" button lets players spawn 3 seeds instantly for a small currency cost.

### 2. Merge (Satisfying Progression)
Matching adjacent crops triggers a merge, evolving them into higher tiers. 
- Visual feedback: animations + color gradients
- Audio feedback: satisfying chime sounds
- Reward feedback: +score, +coins popups

Merging is the core engagement loop. Each merge feels rewarding and drives toward the next goal.

**Monetization Hook:** Upgrades increase merge rewards. Players buy upgrades to feel progression faster.

### 3. Earn (Multiple Reward Streams)
Every merge generates:
- Score (progression toward level goals)
- Coins (currency for upgrades and boosts)
- Combo multipliers (incentivizes consecutive merges)

Multiple reward types keep the feedback loop engaging.

### 4. Upgrade (Long-Term Progression)
Three upgrade types:
- **Water:** +20% coin generation
- **Soil:** +25% merge rewards
- **Luck:** +15% rare spawn chance

Each upgrade has escalating costs, creating a meaningful progression path that takes 10–20+ sessions to fully explore.

### 5. Level (Meta-Goal)
Players progress through levels by reaching score targets. Each level increases difficulty and unlocks new rewards. This keeps goals fresh and gives players a sense of advancement.
