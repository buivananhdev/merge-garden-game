## Slide 3: Game Mechanics

**Title:** Core Gameplay Loop

**Mechanics:**
1. **Spawn** – Tap cells to place seeds
2. **Merge** – Match adjacent crops to evolve them (🌱 → 🌿 → 🌼 → ... → ✨)
3. **Earn** – Coins, score, and combo multipliers
4. **Upgrade** – Buy shop items to boost production and rewards
5. **Progress** – Level up through score goals

**Why It Works:**
- Easy to learn in seconds
- Deep progression keeps players engaged
- Multiple reward streams (coins, score, combos, upgrades)
- Natural monetization hooks

**Visuals:**
- Screenshot sequence showing each step
- Arrow flow diagram: Spawn → Merge → Earn → Upgrade → Progress
